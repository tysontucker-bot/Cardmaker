Symbol Finder

Find ARASAAC pictograms for a word list, pick the ones you want, and download them as one zip file. Upload that zip to the Symbol library in Group ITT Log.

You can paste a card set’s items. Pairs like dog -> puppy search for both words.

Pictograms by Sergio Palao, property of the Government of Aragón, from ARASAAC, shared under CC BY-NC-SA.
